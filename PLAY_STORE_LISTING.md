## Store Descriptions

### Short Description (up to 80 characters)
Professional teleprompter with voice follow, floating overlay, and offline privacy.

### Full Description
Deliver speeches, presentations, lectures, and video recordings with complete confidence using PMPTR Teleprompter — a clean, modern, and completely free Android teleprompter utility.

Designed specifically for public speakers, content creators, keynote presenters, educators, and online meeting participants, PMPTR Teleprompter focuses on flawless script delivery with zero distractions and total privacy.

---

### Key Features

★ **REAL ANDROID FLOATING OVERLAY**
Display a movable, resizable floating teleprompter window over ANY other application. Read your presentation scripts while recording with your default camera app, hosting Zoom/Teams meetings, or live streaming.

★ **INTELLIGENT VOICE FOLLOW**
Enable Voice Follow to let speech recognition track your pace in real time. The teleprompter automatically scrolls as you speak and naturally pauses when you pause.

★ **PROVEN PMPTR-INSPIRED VISUAL CLARITY**
High-contrast typography, adjustable text size, customizable line spacing, letter spacing, side margins, and reading width. Features clean light and battery-saving dark themes.

★ **MIRROR MODE FOR BEAM-SPLITTER RIGS**
Horizontally flips your script with one tap for compatibility with professional glass beam-splitter teleprompter setups, without mirroring user controls or buttons.

★ **BLANK-LINE INTENTIONAL PAUSES**
Blank lines represent deliberate breathing points and rhetorical pauses. Configure how long the teleprompter pauses at blank lines to maintain natural conversational pacing.

★ **FULL DOCUMENT IMPORT & EXPORT**
Easily import plain-text or Markdown scripts from your device or Google Drive using Android's native Storage Access Framework. Export scripts as `.txt` files anytime.

★ **STRICT OFFLINE-FIRST PRIVACY**
- 100% Free forever
- Zero advertisements or ad SDKs
- No user accounts or login required
- Scripts stored locally on your device
- NO CAMERA PERMISSIONS (record with your preferred camera app or hardware)
- Microphone permission is requested ONLY when you turn on Voice Follow
